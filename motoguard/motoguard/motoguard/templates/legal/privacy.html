Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 June 2026

This policy explains what personal data MotoGuard collects, why, and the rights you have over it. It is written to align with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. This is a template and should be reviewed by a qualified adviser before you rely on it.

1. Who we are

MotoGuard is operated by OpsLab Systems ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@opslabsystems.cloud (replace with your real contact address). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your username, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Profile & location: the town, region and country you enter. We do not require precise GPS coordinates; any coordinates you provide are used only to calculate distance for local alerts and are never shown publicly.
  • Vehicle data: make, model, year, colour, registration, VIN/frame number, description, photos, and last-seen location.
  • Sightings, messages, forum posts and reports that you submit.
  • Alert preferences, including whether you have opted in to stolen-bike email alerts.
  • Technical data: server logs may record your IP address, browser and timestamps for security and troubleshooting.

3. Why we use it and our legal basis

  • To provide your account and the service — performance of a contract with you.
  • To send stolen-bike alerts — your consent (opt-in), which you can withdraw at any time in your profile.
  • To help recover stolen bikes and keep the community safe — our legitimate interests and yours.
  • To secure the service and prevent abuse — legitimate interests.
  • To meet legal obligations — e.g. responding to lawful requests.

4. Vehicle look-ups

When you use the registration look-up, the registration you enter is sent to UK government services — the DVSA MOT History API and the DVLA Vehicle Enquiry Service — to retrieve MOT and tax status. Those services process the request under their own terms and privacy notices.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other users: when a bike is marked stolen (or set public), limited details — make, model, colour, registration, description and an approximate area — are shown to help recovery. Your precise coordinates and contact details are never published.
  • Service providers (processors): our email/SMTP provider (to send alerts and password resets), our hosting provider, and Cloudflare (network and security). They process data on our instructions.
  • Government services: DVSA/DVLA as described above; and police or authorities where lawfully required.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.

6. Cookies and third-party content

We use only strictly necessary cookies — a session cookie to keep you logged in and a security (CSRF) token. We do not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies, so no cookie-consent banner is required for these.

Some pages load fonts, styles, mapping tiles and scripts from third parties (Google Fonts, the Tailwind CDN, unpkg and OpenStreetMap). These providers may receive your IP address when content loads. If you would prefer these be self-hosted to avoid that, contact us.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your personal data is erased and content about other users' bikes is anonymised. Security logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port and object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can:

  • Access & portability: download all your data from your profile ("Download my data").
  • Rectification: edit your profile, email, password and bikes directly.
  • Erasure: permanently delete your account and data from your profile ("Delete account").
  • Withdraw consent: turn off alert emails in your profile at any time.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

9. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. The service is served over HTTPS with security headers and CSRF protection, and access to data is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10. Children

MotoGuard is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be highlighted in the service, and the "last updated" date above will change.

Questions? Email privacy@opslabsystems.cloud. See also our Terms & Conditions.
