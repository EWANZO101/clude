FAQ

Common questions

What needs a plan?

Sending texts, making calls, and online apps such as Chirp, Mail, Bank, Garage, music streaming and internet radio. Receiving texts, texting {{ carrier.number }} and calling emergency numbers always work.

What happens when I run out?

That service pauses until your plan renews. Add a boost or upgrade from your account or the {{ carrier.name }} app and it works straight away.

How does renewal work?

At the end of each period we use your account credit first, then your in-game bank. If there isn't enough, the line pauses until you renew.

I changed plans. Do I need a new eSIM?

No — the eSIM stays on your phone. Plan changes apply instantly.

My phone says SOS.

You don't have an active plan, or your eSIM isn't installed yet. Open Settings › Mobile Service on your phone to see why.
