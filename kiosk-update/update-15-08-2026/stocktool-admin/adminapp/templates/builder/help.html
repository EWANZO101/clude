This page lets you design what your kiosk screen looks like — no coding, just clicking and dragging. Here's how:

1. Pick or start a layout

The dropdown at the top holds your saved layouts. Each kiosk can use a different one. Hit New to start a blank one, or Duplicate to copy one you already like.

2. Add sections

The left column is your toolbox. Drag a section (like "Item Grid" or "Category Grid") from the left straight into the middle column — that's your dashboard, top to bottom.

3. Set it up

Click any section in the middle to open its settings on the right. This is where you pick things like which categories show up, how many columns, and what info appears on each card.

4. Reorder and resize

Drag sections up and down in the middle column to change the order. Use the size buttons (Small / Medium / Large / Full) on each section to change how much room it takes up.

5. Made a mistake?

Use the Undo and Redo arrows in the toolbar — they step backward and forward through every change you've made, just like in a word processor.

6. Check it before it goes live

Click Live Preview any time to see exactly what the kiosk will show — safe to click as often as you like, nothing here affects real stock.

7. Save vs. Publish — this is the important bit

Save Draft only saves your work so you can come back to it later. It does not change what any kiosk is currently showing.

Publish is the button that actually sends your layout to the kiosk. Nothing changes on the shop floor until you click Publish.

So: build, preview, tweak, preview again — and only hit Publish once you're happy.

8. Starting over

Reset throws away your current draft and puts back the simple default layout, in case you want a fresh start. Duplicate is the safer option if you just want to try something new without losing what you have.

Where do categories and items come from?

Set those up on the Categories page in the sidebar — create a category, then tick which items or tools belong to it (an item can be in more than one category). Then in Builder Mode, pick that category in a section's settings.
