Sync Log

Stock Audit

Runs automatically every day between 07:00 and 18:00 SAST. If a day is missed, the previous day's audit is backfilled automatically the next time the window opens. A discrepancy is any change in an item's quantity since its last audit -- this system doesn't know your expected restock schedule, so every change is surfaced for review, not just decreases.
