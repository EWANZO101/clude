Backup schedule

Runs on this kiosk's own clock, converted to whichever timezone you pick here — so "2:00 AM" always means 2:00 AM where this kiosk actually is, whatever that ends up being in UTC. A daily local backup stays on the kiosk itself; a cloud backup is additionally uploaded here, where it's kept for up to a year (at most 3 per calendar month, so it doesn't turn into hundreds of near-identical files).

Local Client Portal

A management portal for your own network — local users, roles, sidebar, and inventory — reachable at /client/login on this kiosk's own IP address, separate from its normal login screen. Requires an admin-capable local account with a real password set. Leave blank to turn it off. Takes effect the next time this kiosk checks in — not immediate, and a restart follows automatically once it does.
