WHAT IT IS

A body-worn camera that actually behaves like one

Every officer issued a camera can record their own view as real video, in-game — not a screenshot, not a third-party capture tool. Every finished recording lands in an evidence portal automatically.

Records what they see

The officer's own screen — including the game's HUD and minimap, not the phone or menus.

Goes to Xon Sight

The evidence portal only ever receives a link to the video, never the file itself.

Runs anywhere

ESX, QBCore, Qbox, ox_core, ND, or completely standalone — it finds what's running.

opslabs-bodycam

Starting a recording

Manual

Press F10 (rebindable), type /bodycam, or use the OPS Phone → Bodycam app — start, stop, add a note, see your own footage and who else is live.

Automatic

Starts itself on shots fired, a weapon drawn, or lights & siren above 68 mph. It never stops itself — only going off duty, or the officer, does that.

TriggerFires on
Weapon drawnGun or taser leaves the holster — not just aiming
Shots firedAny weapon discharged, taser included
Lights & sirenDriving at speed with them on — a parked car with lights flashing doesn't count

While recording, the visible camera on the vest shows its red LEDs — everyone nearby can see it's live. A beep marks start and stop, and a double beep every 2 minutes, like the real thing.

Long recordings are cut into parts (3 minutes by default). Each part uploads and becomes its own evidence item as soon as it's done.

THE EVIDENCE SYSTEM

Xon Sight: two pages, one portal

/live

Who's recording right now, with their position and the way they're facing. Updates within about a second of someone starting or stopping — it doesn't wait for the next sync cycle.

/evidence

Every finished part, captioned: officer · job · street · part number · time · what triggered it · any note added from the phone.

Only a link crosses over. The portal never receives the actual video file — just its https address, so the clip has to be reachable on the web, not sent through the game server.

It doesn't give up. If the portal's down or the key's wrong, the same part is retried every 2 minutes, up to 10 times, before it's given up on.

VOICE

Audio comes from OPS Voice, not the video

Recording starts

A listening bot joins the officer's voice channel

→

It keeps every frame

Mic, nearby voices, radio — one stream per speaker

→

Part finishes

ffmpeg mixes and muxes it onto that video part

→

Evidence link

Points at the version with sound

If the voice bridge is down, or the mix fails, the silent video still goes to Xon Sight — the evidence never just disappears, it's just quieter. One honest limit: FiveM gives neither the camera's NUI nor the server the game's own sound, so gunshots, sirens and engines can never be in the recording — only what people say near the officer.

PERFORMANCE

Built to be light on a weak PC

What it doesn't do

  • No separate screenshot resource grabbing frames
  • No second full-scene render of the world
  • No external ffmpeg process running on the client

What it does instead

  • Encodes inside the NUI browser already running the phone
  • Defaults to 1280×720 · 30 fps · 3 Mbps — every number is in config.lua
  • The always-on buffer is a deliberately tiny 320×180 · 8 fps

On genuinely low-end machines, turning the main recording resolution, frame rate or bitrate down costs nothing functionally — the evidence portal, captions and auto-triggers all work exactly the same at any quality setting.

TO BE FAIR

What OPS Bodycam doesn't do yet

Two things the scripts above already have, that OPS Bodycam is still building toward:

Live in-engine viewing · coming soon

spy-bodycam and hype-bodycam let another officer watch a live feed without leaving the game. OPS Bodycam's /live page only shows position and facing today, not video — FiveM gives no cheap, documented way to stream live camera frames out of the game, so this needs solving properly before it ships.

No dashcam yet · coming soon

SX Development, Code Studio, spy-bodycam and hype-bodycam all record from a vehicle too. OPS Bodycam is bodycam-only today, but the same capture pipeline already does the hard part — a dashcam is planned on top of it.
