Privacy policy

Covers this website and the Scheduler iPhone app · Last updated {{ updated }}

Scheduler is a booking and availability tool run by {{ owner_name }}. This page explains what information it handles, why, and who it's shared with. There's no advertising, no tracking and no selling of data.

If you make a booking

When you book a time, we store the name, email address, optional phone number and notes you enter, plus the time you booked. They're used only to run your booking: to confirm it, remind you, and contact you about it. You can view or cancel your booking at any time from the link in your confirmation email.

The Scheduler app

The iPhone app is for the account holder to manage their own schedule. It isn't used by people making bookings, and you can't create an account in it.

  • Sign-in: your email and password are sent securely to this website to sign you in. The app keeps only a sign-in token, in the iPhone's encrypted keychain, and deletes it when you sign out.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app registers a push token for your phone so it can be told about new bookings and cancellations. The token is removed when you sign out. Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service (Expo privacy policy) and Apple's push notification service. They contain only the booking's name and time.
  • Booking details: the app shows the booking information described above, fetched securely from this website. It isn't stored anywhere else.

The app contains no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking.

Where data is kept

Everything is stored on this website's own server and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Booking records are kept for as long as they're needed to run the schedule, and are deleted on request.

Your rights and contact

You can ask to see, correct or delete any information held about you by emailing {{ contact_email }}.
